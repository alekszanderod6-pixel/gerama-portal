Dev C++

C / C++ Programming

A lightweight, free IDE for writing and compiling C and C++ programs. Used in Introduction to Computer Programming and Engineering Computing courses at UENR.

C/C++ Programming Algorithm Design Problem Solving Software Development
C++ is the foundation of embedded systems, robotics and high-performance engineering software. Every engineering student benefits from learning it early.

GNU Octave

MATLAB Alternative · Free

A free, open-source alternative to MATLAB. Used for numerical computation, matrix operations, signal processing and solving engineering mathematics problems.

Numerical Methods Matrix Algebra Signal Processing Data Visualisation
MATLAB skills are required in most engineering jobs. GNU Octave is 100% compatible and completely free – perfect for students who can't afford MATLAB.
Download Setup ~350 MB

OpenFOAM

CFD Simulation · Free & Open Source

The world's leading open-source Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) tool. Used to simulate fluid flow, heat transfer and combustion in mechanical, petroleum and renewable energy engineering.

Fluid Dynamics Heat Transfer Turbulence Modelling Mesh Generation
CFD simulation is a core skill for Mechanical, Petroleum and Renewable Energy engineers. OpenFOAM is used by top universities and companies worldwide – and it's completely free.
