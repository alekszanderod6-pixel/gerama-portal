About GERAMA

Engineering Resources and Academic Materials Association — a student-led initiative empowering engineering excellence at UENR through free, comprehensive academic support.

Who We Are

Built by Students, For Students

GERAMA was born out of a simple but powerful idea — that every engineering student at UENR deserves access to quality academic resources, regardless of their background or circumstances.

What began as a small, informal study group has grown into one of the most active and impactful student-led academic communities at the University of Energy and Natural Resources, Sunyani.

Our History

The story of GERAMA begins in April 2022, when four Electrical and Electronic Engineering students, Gad, Edem, Mawuli, and Rashida — came together with a shared commitment to academic excellence. Their initials formed the original name: Team GERM.

Shortly after, Aaron joined the group, expanding it to Team GERMA. When Alexander (Aleks) came on board, the final letter fell into place, and Team GERAMA was born.

From 2022 through to 2025, the team grew steadily in both membership and impact. Weekly sessions became a cornerstone of academic life for EEE students, so much so that by the time the founding batch approached graduation, not a single member or non-member from EEE Level 400 had missed a meeting.

In those sessions, complex courses were broken down, questions were welcomed, and no one was left behind. GERAMA was never just a study group, it became a family.

Today, GERAMA has evolved into a full digital platform, curating and sharing lecture slides, textbooks, past questions, and video tutorials : all organised by level and course — so every student can study effectively, anytime, anywhere.
