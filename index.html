What We Offer

Everything You Need to Succeed

GERAMA brings together the best academic resources for engineering students at UENR —�� all in one place, completely free.

Lecture Slides & Notes

Comprehensive lecture materials from L100 to L400, organised by course and level for easy access.

Textbooks & References

Curated textbooks and reference materials recommended by lecturers and senior students.

Past Questions & Exams

Years of past exam questions, mid-semester papers and question banks to help you prepare.

Video Tutorials

Step-by-step video explanations of complex topics, created by GERAMA tutors and members.

Peer Community

Connect with fellow students, share resources and get help from seniors and alumni.

GERAMA Connect NEW

DMs, group chats, 24h status stories, reels and group study calls —�� your GERAMA social hub.

Opportunities Hub NEW

Internships, NSS placements, scholarships and jobs —�� curated engineering opportunities for GERAMA members.

Google Drive Backup

All materials are also available on Google Drive as an alternative access point.
