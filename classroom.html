📖 How to use the Study Planner
Tap to expand · 5 easy steps
1
Check Upcoming Deadlines
The top banner auto-fetches your assignment and quiz deadlines from Classroom. Stay ahead — don't wait for reminders!
2
Add Study Goals
Type a goal (e.g. "Revise Chapter 3 — Circuit Analysis") and press Enter or ＋. Tick it off when done to track your progress.
3
Use the Pomodoro Timer
25 min focused study → 5 min break. Research shows this boosts retention by 40%. Press Start, focus on one goal, then take your break.
4
Jot Down Quick Notes
Use Quick Notes to capture formulas, reminders or ideas instantly. Notes are auto-saved to your device.
5
Evening & Morning Reminders
Every evening 8–12 pm: plan tomorrow's study session. Every morning 5–8 am: review today's goals. A 🔔 badge appears on the Planner tab to remind you.
