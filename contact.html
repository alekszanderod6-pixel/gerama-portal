Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get most often.

How do I access study materials?
Go to the Resources page, select your academic level (L100–L400), then browse by course and material type. All materials are free to view and download.
Can I upload my own study materials?
Yes! On the Resources page, click "Upload Resources". Fill in the details and submit your file. Our team reviews all submissions before publishing them to the site.
Are the materials available offline?
Yes. Click the "Download" button on any material to save it to your device for offline access. You can also access everything via our Google Drive folder.
How do I join the GERAMA community?
Send us a message via WhatsApp at +233 55 574 9497 or email us at gerama.uenr@gmail.com. We'll add you to our student community group.
I can't find materials for my course. What should I do?
Use the "Request Academic Assistance" form above or contact us directly. We'll either point you to existing resources or work on adding them to the platform.
Is GERAMA only for engineering students?
Currently our materials focus on engineering programs at UENR, but we welcome all students. We're working on expanding to other faculties.
